Welcome to _Riverbanks_, a climate fiction series by Seapunk Studios.

_Riverbanks_ explores the idea of _ecoarchy_ or nature-first institutions; particularly, through
the notion of a _river-bank_: a bank or financial institution _owned_ by a river or an ecological
entity.

Conceived in early 2026, the first edition of _Riverbanks_ climate fiction premiered as an
eponymous comic exhibition at Bangkok Climate Action Week 2026 from 5-10 October, 2026.

A light web-friendly version of this exhibition will be released here (riverbanks.lol) in November
2026 as a browser-readable comic titled _Riverbanks Volume 1: The Great Pluriversalization_.

Several subsequent ideas (exhibition, translation, publication, etc.) have been suggested. We'll
update here with news. In the meantime, feel free to reach out with further suggestions
[here](#TODO-link).

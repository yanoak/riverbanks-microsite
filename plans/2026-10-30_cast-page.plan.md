# F5: the cast page

## Context

- The [MVP roadmap](2026-10-29_mvp-roadmap.plan.md) has F5 as `/cast`: one sentence and a portrait per
  character, shareable as a whole and per character (`/cast#taro`). The mockup is board 6 on the
  [MVP mockups](https://claude.ai/artifact/5N9u5BM2rdNbbjKcEDRKor) canvas: a three-column grid of
  cards, each with a round portrait, the name, the act and a one-liner, plus Share the cast.
- **Only the main cast with speaking roles** (Yan, 30 Oct). Who speaks comes from the speech,
  thought, shout and whisper lines in each act's script in
  `riverbanks-generator/content/drafts/`, and from the canon for the Prologue and Act One:

  | Act | Speakers (lines) |
  |---|---|
  | Two (*The Youngest Delegate*) | Ismahan 8, Faris 6, Sultan 4, Hamzah 4; a soldier, an older woman, a fisher 1 each |
  | Three (KAE) | Nusa 10, Ismahan 9, Faris 9, Darya 9, Hamzah 8; Siratana, Patika, the two commanders 2 each; others 1 |
  | Four (*The Year It Snowed*) | Ya 13, Ismahan 13, Dew 2 |
  | Five (*A Visa for a Hilsa*) | Ya 11, Jalal 10, Shapla 8, the drone 4; others 1 |
  | Epilogue (*River of Seeds*) | Ismahan 23, Teja 14, Biret 5, Taro 2 |

  In: Darya, Nusa, Ismahan, Sultan Nuh, Tengku Hamzah, Tengku Faris, Dew, Ya, Taro, Jalal,
  Shapla, Teja and Biret.
  Out:
  - minor speakers, those with one or two lines in a single act (Siratana, Patika, the
    commanders, the drone);
  - Soraya, who has no lines;
  - Maya and Jentayu, from the superseded Moving House epilogue that board 6 still lists.
- **Portraits:**
  - The story network's portraits are public in Supabase (`network-portraits/riverbook/<cast
    member id>`), one or more per character, by age. Their URLs are recorded in the
    `story_network` row.
  - Taro has none in the network, but the style profile's cast has her at three ages (kitten,
    cat, beaing), each a sheet in the same bucket. The cast page uses the cat. (A first version
    used her flat reference drawing in `art/taro/refs`, which didn't match the others.)
- **One-liners:** board 6's drafts came from `riverbook.json` before the canon settled. Some are
  stale: Teja's is from Moving House, and "Gross Delish Index" is now Gross Deliciousness Index.
  The new ones are written from the current summaries, for Sam to approve.

## Goal

`/cast` shows the thirteen speaking characters in order of first appearance, each with a portrait,
their name, the acts they're in and one sentence. The page can be shared whole, with its own link
card, and each character has a link of their own (`/cast#ismahan`) that scrolls to and marks their
card. Cast appears in the site menu.

## Approach

- **Content as data:** `src/content/cast.ts`, one entry per character: id, name, acts, line,
  portrait file, and portrait alt text. It's hand-written, not generated from
  `riverbook.json`: the network covers forty entries and its summaries run to paragraphs. The
  page needs thirteen, with one short sentence each, which Sam edits.
- **Portraits copied, not hotlinked:** `scripts/cast-portraits.sh` downloads each chosen
  portrait (and takes Taro's from `art/`), crops it square and writes
  `static/cast/<id>.webp` at 400 px.
  - The portraits are at the age of each character's main appearance: Ismahan at 18, Ya at 20,
    Teja at 11, the Summit speaker for Nusa, the General for Hamzah.
  - The same script composes the page's link card, a 1200×630 grid of the portraits on paper:
    `static/cast/card.jpg`.
  - Rejected: hotlinking Supabase. A re-save of the network re-versions the URLs, and the site
    should not depend on the generator's storage being up.
- **Page:** `SiteHeader` as on `/credits`, then the title, a line of intro, and Share the cast.
  - Share the cast opens the share sheet where there is one, else copies the link.
  - Below that, a grid of cards (three columns on wide screens, two on a tablet, one on a phone).
  - Each card is an `<article id="<id>">`. Its name links to `#<id>`, and a small Copy link button
    copies `/cast#<id>`.
  - `:target` marks the card a link opened, with a yellow ring.
- **Menu:** `CAST_URL` in `src/lib/site.ts` becomes `/cast`, so Cast shows in the header and menu.

## Keyboard interaction

1. **Tab order:** the header, Share the cast, then each card's name link and Copy link in turn.
2. **Shortcuts:** none.
3. **Focus:**
   - Copy link stays focused and reads "Link copied" for a few seconds.
   - Following `#id` moves focus to that card's heading link, so a screen reader announces the
     character.
